Broke It Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The Broke It app is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Broke It YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos to YouTube and to read basic information about the owner's own channel and uploads. It uses this only to upload new Broke It videos as private, confirm they went to the correct channel, and avoid duplicate uploads.

What the app stores and shares

The app stores its Google sign-in token privately on the owner's own computer. It does not collect information about viewers or anyone else, does not sell or share any data, and does not use data for advertising.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions. The locally stored token can be deleted at any time.
